# F3: accountable decisions and enforceable approval limits

Leaders can approve, return, or record **Not building**, with a concrete reason bound to the reviewed spec and frozen packet. Members can read the decision history and each reviewed snapshot after later revisions. Restricted-evidence receipts identify whom to ask without publishing the source. A later decision can record what changed; no appeal queue or automatic escalation is added.

Approval sets a dollar cap, independent-helper quorum, additional build-turn limit, and time window. The server stamps the expiry; replay refuses a contradictory duration. The worker checks expiry before starting, before external dispatch, and before delivery, and stops late work cleanly. The gateway independently refuses expired authority. All turns consume the same approved dollar budget.

Research has six fixed categories. Operator adapters are a code seam and ship disconnected; refreshing records that nothing was checked. Failed adapters report failure without exposing exception text. A client cannot submit research results or connector configuration. SEND embeds the latest research snapshot and any clearly labeled, unverified OP cost/time estimate. Fork packets name their source.

Original SEE records, including fork originals, and decision records refuse product erasure. Authors can remove their own content. Erasing another person's content requires a listed privacy/legal ground and a note, with an attributed tombstone. Research and handoff-note erasure preserve replay.

Schema 8 includes the new event vocabulary and rebuilds derived contexts. The prior-schema golden fixture exercises upgrade and backup behavior. The machine copy is synchronized to the demo; a research story exercises accepted and refused records through both implementations.
