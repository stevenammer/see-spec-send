      What's real and what isn't

      • Real: the state machine. It's a generated copy of core/machine.js, and a check fails if the copy drifts.
      • Close, but smaller: the log. It's append-only and hash-chained like the real store, with authority looked up from the fixed roster. It lives in memory, not in a database.
      • Not here: sign-in, invites, sessions, the SQLite store, the leader password step-up, rate limits, builder runs and a playable try. Nobody here is a real person. One item at a time.
      • Your browser only: this page makes no requests after it loads and sets no cookies. It stores nothing unless you tick the box below.
