# Vision

See / Spec / Send is an open-source way for an organization to turn the problems everyone already knows about into small, checked, capped experiments, and to remember what it learned. The working umbrella name is **E.F.F.O.R.T. for Organizations**. Names are still provisional and nothing here is a trademark claim.

## The loop

See a problem, spec a fix, send it for a real decision, try a small bounded version, let the people who do the work say whether it helps, then keep what was learned. The loop does not end there: when something new is learned later, earlier work gets a second look.

## Continuous reanalysis (planned)

New results inside the system, such as a build that helped, a closed case or new evidence, will be checked against earlier decisions and work. A match becomes a separate, dated update linked to the old case. The old record never changes. People may then choose to make a new decision based on the new information. Nobody is asked to change their mind; they are asked whether the new information changes what they would decide today. Updates also point sideways, to other teams that might benefit and to unusual setups that could break a fix. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for what exists today.

## Principles

- An objection is a request for more information. Some are conditions that can be met, some are hard limits, and many are simply a short stated reason, which is enough.
- Earlier decisions stay on the record as they were made. New information leads to a new decision, never an edited one.
- AI findings are hypotheses until checked, and are always labeled as AI.
- A frozen record stays frozen. Updates are separate, linked and dated.
- People decide and people approve spending. An AI finding is never an approval.
- The same standard of evidence applies at every level of the organization, including to the people at the top.
- No scores, rankings or quotas for people. Useful no's, useful yes's and useful investigations all count.
- Private stays private. Mentoring is between people and the system does not track it.
- Your data, your models, your tools. Storage, model and coding agent should be the organization's choice.
- "Public" means visible to the appropriate audience inside the organization, not the internet.

## What it is not

Not a chatbot, not an AI manager that tells people what to do, not surveillance, and not only an app builder. Sometimes the best outcome is a setting that already exists, a process change, or a clear no.

## Where it runs

The forum comes first as the complete experience. Other places people already work, starting with Microsoft Teams, come after the forum is complete and will use the same rules.
